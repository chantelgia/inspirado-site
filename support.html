Common questions

Can I save from any app?

You can save from TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Pinterest, and websites. Some platforms (Instagram especially) only share a link — your save always keeps the original link so you can tap back to the creator anytime.

Which videos work best with the AI?

YouTube tutorials work best, because the AI watches the video. TikToks and Reels work when someone talks through the steps or the caption lists them, and how-to and recipe websites work great too. Silent reels with only music and text on screen are the hardest. For those, take screenshots of the steps and use 📸 Add from screenshots.

Can I add steps or materials myself?

Yes. Open the project and scroll to Materials, Tools or Steps, tap Add material…, Add tool… or Add step…, and type. Tap ✎ on any line to edit it. If the AI can't read a link, tap 📸 Add from screenshots and it will read your screenshots instead, or save the tutorial and fill it in whenever you like.

Do you credit the original creator?

Always. We never re-host anyone's video. Every saved source shows a prominent "View Original" that links back to the creator's post.

Is InspiraDo free?

Yes. Saving tutorials, making projects, decks, shopping lists, Follow Mode and search are free, always. Right now AI-built projects are free too. When InspiraDo Pro launches, free accounts will include 7 AI-built projects and Pro will include 50 a month. Everyone who joins before then gets 6 months of Pro free (see Founding Members).

Do I keep my projects if I stop Pro?

Yes, always. Everything you've saved and made is yours to keep: every project, its steps, materials and shopping list, your decks, notes, photos and Made shelf. Only new AI-built projects count toward the limit, and when Pro ends you start with a fresh 7 free AI projects.

How do I delete my account?

Open the app, go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all your data.

Will there be an Android version?

iPhone comes first. Android is on the roadmap.
