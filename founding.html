Founding Members

Last updated: November 13, 2026

InspiraDo is brand new, and the people who join us now are shaping it. As a thank-you, everyone who signs up before InspiraDo Pro launches becomes a Founding Member.

What you get

  • 6 months of InspiraDo Pro, free. Your free period starts the day Pro launches.
  • A Founding Member badge in the app, for good.

Who qualifies

  • Anyone who creates an InspiraDo account (on iPhone or in the browser) before Pro launches. We'll announce the Pro launch date in the app and on this page.
  • One Founding Member free period per account.

No card, no surprise charges

You don't need a payment method to get your free 6 months, and you won't be charged automatically when they end. Afterwards you can choose to subscribe to Pro, or simply keep using InspiraDo on the free plan.

Free vs. Pro

  • Free, always: save unlimited tutorials, organize them into decks, create projects, use shopping lists, Follow Mode, search and your Made history, plus 7 AI-built projects.
  • Pro: up to 50 AI-built projects every month (turning a link or screenshots into steps, and combining videos into one plan). Pro is planned at $4.99/month or $39.99/year; you'll always see the current price before you subscribe.

How many AI projects?

Pro, including your free Founding Member months, includes up to 50 AI-built projects per month, and the count resets on the 1st. That's far more than most people use, and it keeps InspiraDo sustainable for everyone. Saving tutorials is never limited.

The fine print

  • The offer is tied to your InspiraDo account and can't be transferred or exchanged for cash.
  • If we ever change this offer, it won't affect Founding Members who have already qualified.
  • These terms sit alongside our Terms and Privacy Policy.
