Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE] · Draft — review with counsel before launch

InspiraDo is operated by Wise Guys Collective LLC ("we", "us"). It's a personal organizer that lets you save how-to content and turn it into projects. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. We designed the app to collect as little as possible.

What we collect

  • Account info: your email address and display name, so you can sign in and sync across devices.
  • Your content: the projects, saved links, notes, materials, steps, and photos you create in the app.
  • Saved source links: when you save a tutorial, we store its original URL and public attribution (creator name, thumbnail where the platform provides one). We do not download or re-host creators' videos.
  • Basic technical data: standard logs needed to run the service securely.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't show third-party ads.
  • We don't post anything on your behalf.

How your data is stored

Your data is stored securely with our infrastructure provider (Supabase) and protected so that only your account can access your own content. Connections use HTTPS encryption.

AI features

If you use features that turn a saved link into structured steps, the text we can access (captions, descriptions, transcripts, or on-device audio you choose to process) may be sent to an AI service solely to generate that result for you. We don't use your content to train models.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: you can permanently delete your account and all associated data from within the app (Settings → Delete account).
  • Access/export: contact us to request a copy of your data.

Children

InspiraDo is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We'll update this page if our practices change, and revise the date above.

Contact

Questions? Email support@inspirado.app.
