Support

We're a small team and we read every message.

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email us any time at support@inspirado.app — we aim to reply within a couple of days.

Common questions

Can I save from any app?

You can save from TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Pinterest, and websites. Some platforms (Instagram especially) only share a link — your save always keeps the original link so you can tap back to the creator anytime.

Do you credit the original creator?

Always. We never re-host anyone's video. Every saved source shows a prominent "View Original" that links back to the creator's post.

How do I delete my account?

Open the app, go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all your data.

Will there be an Android version?

iPhone comes first. Android is on the roadmap.

Still stuck?

Email support@inspirado.app and tell us what happened — screenshots help.
