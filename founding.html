Founding Members

Last updated: November 19, 2026

InspiraDo is brand new, and the people who joined us early are shaping it. InspiraDo Pro launched on November 19, 2026. As a thank-you, everyone who created an account by the end of that day is a Founding Member.

What you get

  • 6 months of InspiraDo Pro, free, starting from Pro's launch.
  • A Founding Member badge in the app, for good.

Who qualifies

  • Anyone who created an InspiraDo account (on iPhone or in the browser) by 11:59 pm Pacific Time on November 19, 2026, the day Pro launched. If you signed up late that day, Pro is added to your account the next morning.
  • One Founding Member free period per account.

No card, no surprise charges

You don't need a payment method to get your free 6 months, and you won't be charged automatically when they end. Afterwards you can choose to subscribe to Pro, or simply keep using InspiraDo on the free plan.

Free vs. Pro

  • Free, always: save unlimited tutorials, organize them into decks, create projects, use shopping lists, Follow Mode, search and your Made history, plus 7 AI-built projects.
  • Pro: up to 50 AI-built projects every month (turning a link or screenshots into steps, materials and tools). Pro is $4.99/month or $39.99/year in the US; you'll always see the current price before you subscribe.

How many AI projects?

Pro, including your free Founding Member months, includes up to 50 AI-built projects per month, and the count resets on the 1st. That's far more than most people use, and it keeps InspiraDo sustainable for everyone. Saving tutorials is never limited.

Each tutorial the AI reads for you (a link, or a set of screenshots) counts as one AI-built project. Reading the same link again doesn't count, combining tutorials you've already added into one plan doesn't count, and writing or editing steps yourself never counts.

The fine print

  • The offer is tied to your InspiraDo account and can't be transferred or exchanged for cash.
  • If we ever change this offer, it won't affect Founding Members who have already qualified.
  • These terms sit alongside our Terms and Privacy Policy.
